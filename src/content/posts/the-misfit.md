From the beginning, from the title of being a girl who always tended to be a misfit, I have strived to fit into a particular circle.

However, I have consistently found myself being excluded from these circles. This used to evoke feelings of pity within me until I came to the realization that some individuals are not meant to fit into existing circles; instead, they are here to create their own. It has been some time since I had this clarity.

Up until today, I do not feel like I belong anywhere, and I no longer harbor the desire to fit in. I have discovered a sense of belonging within myself. I have come to understand that I can create a mindful circle around me, elevating the vibrations to create a warm and enchanting atmosphere.

On my journey to becoming more conscious each day, I choose to disregard the label "MISFIT."
