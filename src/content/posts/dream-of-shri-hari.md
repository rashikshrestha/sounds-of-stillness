Two weeks ago, I had an intense and unforgettable dream of Shri Mahavishnu.🔥\
It was unlike anything I had ever experienced before ...deeply divine and overwhelmingly beautiful.\
In the dream, He explained everything about illusion ... how He creates Maya, and He even showed me Maya in a way I could feel and witness.

I felt completely calm, deeply relaxed, and at peace.\
He told me, with infinite grace🪷

“Surrender everything, for all is happening by My will. Just surrender.”🕊

Those words touched me like never before.

There was a technique He shared ...how He creates Maya  and I could hold onto the memory for just about 15 to 20 seconds after waking.\
But then, it faded. \
And I feel... if I could only remember that moment, that method, I would rise higher, beyond.\
It felt like the answer to everything I’ve ever sought.\
In that moment, I was in ecstasy  like I couldn’t even contain myself. It was as though all the answers were revealed.

It happened around 2 a.m.\
From that night onward, something in me shifted.\
I became deeply devoted to Shri Hari ...\
To His divine forms as Shri Ram, Shri Krishna.

Now my heart is completely absorbed in Him.

It was the most intense and sacred dream I’ve ever had.🔥
