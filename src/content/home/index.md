I am a spiritual seeker. I walk the path of Bhakti Yoga, Karma Yoga, Jnana Yoga, and Kriya Yoga — believing all four pillars are essential to becoming a more conscious human being, as guided by Sadhguru. I'm on a journey of conscious living... on the path of Suddhi, on the path of becoming.

I am deeply present in my feminine essence. I love doing Devi Sadhana, understanding the Devi that resides within me, and treating this body as a holy temple. I love doing shringar, wearing sarees, alta, bangles, and more, as I feel deeply rooted in my feminine essence.

This blog is my sacred space where I share my experiences of surrender, devotion, and becoming. I have always been a quiet observer of life, and now I put those reflections into words as a way to express and evolve. I am also a registered nurse by profession, witnessing all forms of human emotions in their raw form, which is shaping me into a more sensitive and aware human being each day.

Each piece here is a small offering... a glimpse into my inward journey.
